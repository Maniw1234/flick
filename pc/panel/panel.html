Welcome to Flick
  1. Install the Flick app on your Android phone (Android 9 or newer): on the phone, open github.com/Maniw1234/flick/releases, download the latest Flick .apk and install it.
  2. Connect the phone and this PC to the same Wi-Fi.
  3. Open Flick on the phone, hold it next to this screen, then press and hold Connect.
  4. Drag a file off the edge of the phone toward the PC.
Or drop files on this window. In File Explorer, right-click a file and choose Send to > Flick (phone).

What’s new in Flick 2.1
  • Computer to computer and phone to phone, with everything Flick does between a phone and a PC.
  • One Connect button: hold it on one device, allow it on the other, tap Same code.
  • No shared Wi-Fi needed: this PC can make one for a phone, and phones find each other over Bluetooth.
  • Every transfer at the top of this window, with how far it’s got.
